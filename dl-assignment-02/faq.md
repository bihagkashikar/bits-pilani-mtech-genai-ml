# FAQ

| #  | Question                                           | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|----|----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | Can I use nn.LSTM and nn.GRU?                      | Yes in Problem 1 tasks 3, 4 and 5 (the comparisons and experiments), where speed matters. Not in Problem 1 task 2, where you write your own cell and use nn.LSTM (and nn.RNN, nn.GRU) only to check it. |
| 2  | Can I use nn.Transformer or nn.MultiheadAttention? | No. Problem 2 uses your own attention, multi-head attention and encoder block throughout; the library versions may only check your code.                                                                |
| 3  | Can I use TensorFlow or Keras?                     | No. PyTorch only.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 4  | Do I need a GPU?                                   | A free Colab T4 is enough. Problem 1 also runs on a laptop CPU. For the language model without a GPU, use d = 64 and 2 layers and say so in the README.                                                 |
| 5  | Which hidden size, width and seeds are mine?       | Look up the last digit of your student ID in the variants table, under Individualisation.                                                                                                               |
| 6  | How many epochs and what learning rate?            | Your choice, but the same budget for every model within a comparison. Use Adam with learning rate 1e-3 as the starting point and state the epoch count in the report.                                   |
| 7  | Can I change the model details (layers, dropout)?  | Yes: number of layers, dropout and the classifier head are yours to choose, as long as the hidden size is your variant's value and RNN, LSTM and GRU get the same training budget.                      |
| 8  | My accuracy is below the sanity range.             | That is acceptable if the method is sound, the tests pass and your analysis explains it. Marks follow method and evidence.                                                                              |
| 9  | My Colab session disconnected.                     | Write results.csv after every run and keep the notebook outputs. If you are short of time, reduce epochs and say so.                                                                                    |
| 10 | What does mean ± std mean here?                    | The mean and the sample standard deviation of the metric over your three seeds.                                                                                                                         |
| 11 | Can I use a different dataset?                     | Problem 1 only, and only with approval; send the request by Wed 11 November 2026, 11:59 IST. Problem 2 uses the datasets given.                                                                         |
